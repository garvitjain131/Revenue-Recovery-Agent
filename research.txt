1. Executive Summary

The proposed product is an AI Revenue Intelligence & Growth Agent for Razorpay merchants.

The core problem is not simply that payments fail. Revenue can leak at multiple stages:

Payment failure → checkout drop-off → subscription failure → overdue invoice → customer churn

A merchant may have all the underlying transaction data but still lack the ability to continuously answer:

Where is revenue being lost?
Why is it being lost?
How much money is at risk?
Which intervention is most likely to recover it?
Should the intervention be automated or require merchant approval?
Did the intervention actually recover incremental revenue?

The proposed agent closes this loop.

Instead of building another isolated abandoned-cart or subscription-recovery agent, the product should be positioned as a Revenue Recovery Orchestrator that sits above individual workflows.

Razorpay already offers specialized agents for abandoned-cart conversion and subscription recovery, making a standalone implementation of either less differentiated. Razorpay's Agent Studio currently includes agents such as Abandoned Cart Conversion, Subscription Recovery, Dispute Responder, Cashflow Forecaster, RTO Shield and Settlement Insights.

"An AI agent that finds the merchant's highest-value revenue leakage across multiple payment workflows, explains the cause, predicts the best recovery action, executes it within merchant-defined guardrails, and proves how much incremental revenue it recovered."

6. Feature 2 — Revenue-at-Risk Scoring

Not every failed payment deserves equal attention.

A ₹199 failed transaction should not necessarily be prioritized over a ₹1.2 lakh B2B invoice.

Proposed model

Calculate:

Revenue Risk Score =
Amount at Risk
× Probability of Recovery
× Customer Value
× Urgency
× Intervention Effectiveness


Example:

Opportunity	Amount	Recovery Probability	Priority
Customer A failed payment	₹800	75%	Low

Subscription renewal	₹12,000	65%	High

The agent therefore optimizes for expected recovered revenue, not simply number of failures.

36. What NOT To Build

Several ideas sound impressive but would weaken the project.

Don't make it only an analytics dashboard

A dashboard does not satisfy the agentic requirement strongly enough.

Don't build only abandoned-cart recovery

Razorpay already has this capability. 
R
Razorpay

Don't build only subscription recovery

Razorpay already has a dedicated Subscription Recovery Agent. 
R
Razorpay

Don't make the LLM responsible for every decision

Financial actions need deterministic validation and guardrails.

Don't claim revenue was recovered merely because a customer paid after a message

Use control/holdout experimentation or clearly label the result as attributed versus observed.

Don't build a huge ML model

The buildathon rewards evidence and working execution. A transparent model with strong evaluation is preferable.
